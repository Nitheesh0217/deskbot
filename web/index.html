How this firm is meant to make money

DeskBot is a systematic directional desk: it chooses long, short or WAIT from rule-based estimates after costs, then keeps the evidence to find out when those estimates are wrong. It is not a market maker (quoting both sides to earn the spread at scale) and does not try to be one.

  • During trading: select only among strategies already approved, with fixed conditions.
  • During research: propose, test on frozen data, and promote a new version only after review. Rules are not rewritten after a loss.
  • Agreement between AI roles is not independent evidence; only measured contributions count (FILTER-1).

The departments are logical roles modelled on how systematic firms describe their process (data, research, portfolio construction, risk, execution, operations), not a claim about any firm's floors.
